# User Guide

## Getting started
1. **Register** — pick a username, a password (6+ characters), and a display name.
2. **Login** — wrong credentials show an error; repeated failures temporarily lock login from your network.
3. Sessions last 15 minutes; after that you are returned to the login page.

## Profile
Open **Profile** from the navigation to edit your display name, avatar URL, "show as online" preference, or change your password (current password required).

## Groups
- **Groups** lists groups you belong to (your role badge: owner / admin / member).
- **Create group** makes you the owner.
- Open a group to see details, the member list, and management actions:
  - Owner/admin can add members, change roles, and remove members.
  - Only owners can delete a group (this removes its tasks, chat, and memberships).
  - Removed members immediately lose access everywhere — including live chat.

## Tasks
- Inside a group, open **Tasks** to create and manage tasks.
- Each task has title, description, priority (low/medium/high/urgent), optional assignee, start/due dates, and status (todo → in progress → completed).
- Owners/admins assign tasks; assignees and creators can update them.
- Use filters/search/sorting on the task list; large lists are paginated.

## Dashboard
- **Dashboard** shows your personal overview: assigned tasks (with overdue count), due within 24 hours, created by you, and unread notifications — each linking to your tasks.
- **Team Productivity by Group** breaks down every group you belong to: total, to do, in progress, completed, overdue, due soon, unassigned, and a completion percentage bar. Click a group name to open it.
- Click the chevron next to a group to expand its **member workload** — each member's assigned-task counts and completion rate, plus an "Unassigned tasks" row, so you can see how work is distributed (and who has overdue items). Click again to collapse.

## Checklists
On a task's detail page, add checklist items, tick them off (shows who completed and when), rename, reorder by editing the order number, or delete them. A progress counter tracks completion.

## Chat & comments
- **Group chat** lives in the group detail page — messages appear instantly for everyone in the group.
- **Task comments** live on each task's detail page.
- Mention teammates with `@username` in a comment/message to notify them directly.
- You can edit or delete your own messages/comments; group owners/admins can delete any message.

## Notifications
The bell icon shows unread count and opens your notifications:
- Types: task assigned to you, task completed, new messages/comments, deadline reminders (24 h before due), and @mentions.
- Click a notification to open the related task/group (it is marked read automatically).
- **Mark all read**, delete individual items, or load older ones.
- Set which types you receive under **Profile → Notifications** (disabled types are never created for you).

## Realtime behaviour
Messages, comments, and notifications arrive instantly while you are logged in. If your connection drops, the app reconnects automatically and refreshes data from the server — nothing is lost, duplicates are filtered.

## On small screens
The top bar adapts to phones: the bell stays visible and the page links (and Logout) move into a menu behind the ☰ button. Tap it to open, tap outside or press Escape to close.

## Logout
Logging out clears your session on that browser tab/device and disconnects realtime updates.
